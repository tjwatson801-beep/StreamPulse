# StreamPulse alpha.50 — section-based stream highlights

Recordings longer than 15 minutes are analyzed in 15-minute sections with one minute of overlap. Each section uses its own typical audio level, allowing peaks in quieter parts of a stream to be suggested alongside louder sections. Duplicate or heavily overlapping suggestions are removed. Full-stream suggestions are shown in recording order, with original timestamps, up to 64 distinct moments.

Recordings of 15 minutes or less retain the previous detector and its eight-suggestion limit. Analysis decodes the audio once; importing smaller files manually is no longer needed to get local volume comparisons.

This is audio-peak detection, not a judgment of humor or clip quality. Review suggestions for context.
